# Press Release

## Headline

IQ Legal introduces automated Purchase Ledger Approval workflows with multi-tier spend authorization hierarchies and digital invoice verification.

## Summary

IQ Legal announces the launch of dedicated Purchase Ledger Approval controls engineered to streamline supplier invoice management and enforce financial governance across law firms. This release introduces configurable, multi-tier approval hierarchies with role- and threshold-based routing, alongside side-by-side digital invoice inspection directly within the cashiering workflow. By eliminating manual, paper- and email-based invoice authorization chains, finance teams eliminate unauthorized expenditure and accelerate month-end ledger reconciliation.

## Problem Statement

Legal finance teams and cashiers face administrative friction and internal control vulnerabilities when managing supplier invoices through manual paper routing and ad-hoc email approvals. Lack of centralized spend threshold enforcement leads to unauthorized overhead commitments, delayed invoice sign-offs, misplaced physical vouchers, and increased risk during statutory audit reviews.

## Solution

Practice & Case Management System (PCMS) delivers an integrated Purchase Ledger Approval module that automates invoice authorization lifecycles. The system provides configurable approval tiers based on nominal code allocations and financial thresholds, automated notifications for designated approvers, side-by-side PDF invoice previews during review, and complete immutable audit trails tracking approval timestamps, reviewer decisions, and delegation histories prior to ledger posting.
