# Legal research inside IQ

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** IQ Practice Case Management System
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
IQ now integrates legal research from Harvey, Legora, and LexisNexis—enabling fee earners to conduct research without leaving the platform

## Summary
IQ now supports integrated legal research capabilities, allowing fee earners to access third-party legal research providers including Harvey, Legora, and LexisNexis directly within the platform. This release marks the first time IQ bridges the practice of law and the business of law, eliminating tool-switching friction and accelerating matter research cycles for mid-market and large UK legal practices.

## Problem Statement
Fee earners across mid-market and large legal practices currently lose significant billable time switching between IQ and external legal research platforms such as Harvey, Legora, and LexisNexis. This fragmented workflow interrupts research momentum, increases operational friction, and fragments the matter context—particularly for solicitors, advocates, and paralegals working across complex commercial litigation, family law, and conveyancing matters. The absence of integrated research capability forces practitioners to maintain multiple tool contexts simultaneously, reducing cognitive clarity and matter velocity.

## Solution
IQ now embeds legal research capabilities through native integrations with Harvey, Legora, and LexisNexis, allowing fee earners to initiate and conduct legal research directly within the matter workspace. Research results flow seamlessly into matter documentation workflows, eliminating context-switching overhead and preserving the continuity of legal work. This capability extends IQ's scope beyond pure case management into active legal practice support—unifying both the business of law (accounting, compliance, workflow automation) and the practice of law (research-informed matter strategy and drafting).
