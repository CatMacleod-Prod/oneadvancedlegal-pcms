# Time Capture joins the portfolio

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Time Capture
**Version:** 1.0

## Headline
Time Capture eliminates manual timesheet building by automatically drafting fee earner timesheets from real-time work activity tracking.

## Summary
Time Capture joins the PCMS portfolio as a lightweight time tracking and timesheet automation solution designed for mid-market and large legal practices. By deploying quiet background Time Finders that monitor what fee earners are actively working on, Time Capture automatically drafts accurate, detailed timesheets for review and approval—eliminating the need for fee earners to reconstruct their day from memory.

## Problem Statement
Fee earners in busy legal practices currently waste significant unbillable time reconstructing timesheets at the end of each day from fragmented memory and incomplete records. This manual, error-prone process results in incomplete time capture, underrecorded billable hours, and reduced profitability. Practice managers lack visibility into how time is actually spent across matters and clients, making it difficult to identify which work streams are truly profitable and where operational inefficiencies exist.

## Solution
Time Capture sits alongside PCMS Core and passively monitors fee earner activity in real time through Time Finders—background processes that track what people are actively working on across email, documents, matter systems, and other work applications. At timesheet submission time, Time Capture presents a draft timesheet pre-populated with the actual work recorded, requiring only fee earner review and approval. Firms gain complete, accurate time records without imposing daily logging burden, while practice leaders gain granular insight into time allocation and matter profitability.
