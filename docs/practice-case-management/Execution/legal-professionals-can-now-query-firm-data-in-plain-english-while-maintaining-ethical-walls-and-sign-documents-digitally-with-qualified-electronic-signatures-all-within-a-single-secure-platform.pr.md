# Ask your data safely

## Headline
Legal professionals can now query firm and matter data in plain English while maintaining strict ethical walls across practices—all within a single, secure platform.

## Summary
This release enables fee earners and legal professionals to ask natural language questions about their firm's data without breaching ethical walls, ensuring users only access information they are permitted to see. The AI-driven query engine operates seamlessly across desktop and web workspaces, accelerating matter intelligence while maintaining dual-jurisdiction compliance across England, Wales, and Scotland.

## Problem Statement
Legal professionals struggle with severe data access bottlenecks: retrieving operational and matter intelligence requires technical intermediaries or cumbersome legacy query tools, consuming billable time and creating reporting delays. Furthermore, ethical walls and information barriers are difficult to enforce consistently during manual data access, exposing practices to compliance risk and regulatory scrutiny.

## Solution
The platform introduces an AI-driven natural language query interface that translates plain English questions into secure data retrieval, enforcing ethical wall rules at query time so users see only permitted matter information. Embedded directly within the PCMS Core Application, the engine delivers instant insights across matters, financial metrics, and timelines while maintaining full audit logging compliant with SRA and Law Society of Scotland standards.
