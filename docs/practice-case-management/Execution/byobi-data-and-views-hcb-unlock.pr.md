# Press Release

## Headline

OneAdvanced expands BYOBI analytics for legal practices with ten new native Snowflake financial models

## Summary

Practice & Case Management extends its Bring Your Own Business Intelligence (BYOBI) offering, introducing 10 additional ready-to-use analytical models alongside enhanced billing dispatch tracking. This major expansion unlocks comprehensive financial reporting across time recording, fee-earner adjustments, unbilled and billed expenses, fee billing, payment allocations, write-offs, client money ledgers, and aged debt. Legal finance leaders and practice managers can now connect their preferred reporting tools—including Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, and Looker—directly to Snowflake to achieve instant, firm-wide financial insights with verified penny-perfect accuracy and full dual-jurisdiction regulatory compliance.

## Problem Statement

Legal finance teams and practice managers struggle to gain timely, end-to-end visibility into their practice's financial performance. Core financial data—including unbilled disbursements, fee allocations, write-offs, aged debtors, and statutory client money balances—remains locked behind manual data requests, slow reporting processes, and fragmented spreadsheets. This reporting friction delays critical billing decisions, increases compliance overhead, and prevents leadership from getting a complete, consolidated view across all practice areas.

## Solution

This release supercharges existing BYOBI capabilities by adding 10 pre-built, high-performance financial data models directly within Snowflake. Law firms can now connect Power BI, Tableau, or Looker to access complete, point-in-time financial metrics—from matter-level fee realization to statutory client account balances—with sub-second query speeds. The solution incorporates live bill dispatch tracking, automated accuracy validation ensuring 100% financial consistency with the core practice management system, and built-in compliance checks aligned with Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) and Law Society of Scotland requirements.
