# Frontier certification and architectural upgrade, wave three

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** PCMS, Legal Forms, Time Capture
**Version:** Wave 3 – Q4 FY26

## Headline
PCMS, Legal Forms, and Time Capture Achieve Frontier Certification; Forms Migration to IQ Accelerates Deep Customer Adoption

## Summary
Wave Three of the Frontier certification programme certifies Practice Case Management System (PCMS), Legal Forms, and Time Capture modules against Frontier architectural standards, ensuring cloud-native scalability and enterprise-grade reliability. Legal Forms migration into the IQ platform provides customers with unified, modernized legal document drafting and assembly, removing fragmentation and enabling deeper integration across the PCMS ecosystem. This release establishes the foundational architecture necessary for customers to adopt IQ-powered workflows at scale across matter lifecycle, accounts, and compliance operations.

## Problem Statement
Mid-market and large UK legal practices operate across fragmented legacy systems and desktop plugins that create operational silos, slow matter throughput, and increase compliance risk. Legal Forms remain decoupled from core case management, forcing fee earners to switch between disconnected interfaces. Time tracking lacks seamless integration with matter accounting and billing workflows. These architectural gaps prevent practices from realizing the full business value of a unified, cloud-native legal operating system and constrain their ability to scale concurrent user capacity and cross-border matter velocity.

## Solution
Frontier certification validates PCMS, Legal Forms, and Time Capture against cloud-native architecture standards, ensuring secure multi-tenancy, sub-200ms transaction latency, and 100+ concurrent user scalability across England, Wales, and Scotland. Legal Forms migration into IQ unifies document assembly, legal precedent drafting, and matter templates within a single, modernized interface integrated directly with case management workflows. Time Capture integration with core accounting modules enables real-time matter realization, automated fee-earner utilization reporting, and seamless billing cycle closure—eliminating manual overhead and protecting profit margins.
