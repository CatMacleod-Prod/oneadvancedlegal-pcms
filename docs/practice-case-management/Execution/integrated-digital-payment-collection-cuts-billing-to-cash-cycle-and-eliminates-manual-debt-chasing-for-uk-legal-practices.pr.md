# Cash collection with digital payments

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** PCMS Core Application – Client Billing & Payment Module
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Integrated Digital Payment Collection Cuts Billing-to-Cash Cycle and Eliminates Manual Debt Chasing for UK Legal Practices

## Summary
Legal practices can now embed integrated payment capabilities directly into client billing workflows, enabling clients to pay invoices in seconds through a secure digital interface. This release eliminates manual debt collection overhead, dramatically shortens cash conversion cycles, and particularly benefits firms with high-volume, lower-value matters where payment delays severely impact cash flow.

## Problem Statement
UK legal practices—especially those managing high volumes of smaller matters or serving cost-conscious clients—currently suffer from extended billing-to-cash cycles due to manual invoicing, email follow-ups, and delayed client payment processing. Partners and legal cashiers spend hours each week chasing late payments, invoices sit unpaid for weeks, and firms with thin margins on smaller matters experience acute cash flow strain. The reliance on manual payment workflows, paper cheques, and offline bank transfers creates operational friction, administrative overhead, and unpredictable revenue timing.

## Solution
The Cash Collection with Digital Payments module integrates a vetted payment service provider directly into the PCMS client portal and billing workflow. Clients receive digital invoices with embedded one-click payment links; firms gain real-time payment reconciliation via Open Banking integrations and automated ledger updates. Manual debt chasing is replaced by configurable payment reminders, and firms see immediate cash-in-bank visibility without manual bank reconciliation or accounting overhead.

## Customer Impact
