# One connected experience in IQ

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Intelligent Experience (IQ)
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Legal, HR, and payroll practitioners access PCMS, Time Capture, and cross-practice functions through a single unified Intelligent Experience, eliminating login fragmentation and manual handoffs.

## Summary
For the first time, legal practices can access Practice Case Management System (PCMS), Time Capture, and integrated HR and payroll capabilities through one seamless Intelligent Experience, alongside existing Office 365 and data capabilities. This unified interface dramatically reduces user friction, eliminates redundant authentication, and automatically surfaces integration points that previously required manual navigation and coordination between disconnected systems.

## Problem Statement
Legal practice teams today operate across fragmented systems—PCMS for case management, separate time capture tools, distinct HR and payroll platforms, and standalone Office 365 environments. This landscape creates cognitive overload, repeated login cycles, manual data handoffs, and operational delays. Fee earners, administrators, and finance teams waste hours switching contexts and reconciling data across siloes, increasing administrative overhead and reducing billable utilization. The lack of a unified entry point forces practitioners to maintain mental maps of where each task lives, introducing error and slowing critical workflows like matter setup, timesheet submission, and financial reconciliation.

## Solution
IQ delivers a single, role-aware Intelligent Experience that integrates PCMS, Time Capture, HR, payroll, Office 365, and data analytics into one cohesive interface. The platform automatically detects which systems and workflows are relevant to each user's role, surfaces them in context, and pre-populates integration points so practitioners can move seamlessly between case work, time logging, compliance tasks, and financial reporting without re-authentication or manual data transfer. Office 365 capabilities—email filing, document co-authoring, and SharePoint integrations—are woven into the core experience, not bolted on. Users authenticate once and work continuously across all practice functions.

This release represents the convergence of PCMS Core, Time Capture, HR/payroll integrations, and Office 365 orchestration into a single front-end experience. The underlying technical architecture (PCMS microservices, Document Management & Integration Service, and ACA containerization) remains unchanged; IQ is primarily a UX and integration orchestration layer that unifies navigation, authentication, and workflow surfacing. This aligns with the Design Vision of "Frictionless Matter & Cross-Border Practice Velocity" and "Seamless Desktop, Office 365 & Dual-Outlook Continuity" from the UX strategy.
