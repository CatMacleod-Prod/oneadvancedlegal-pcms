# Enhanced client communications and digital execution through WhatsApp, SMS, and e-Signatures

## Headline
Legal firms can now message clients directly via WhatsApp and SMS and execute documents with qualified electronic signatures directly from matter workflows.

## Summary
We are releasing integrated WhatsApp, SMS messaging, and digital document execution capabilities within PCMS workflows, enabling legal practitioners to communicate and sign documents through secure, client-friendly channels. Firms can request documents, chase onboarding steps, execute agreements using standard or Qualified Electronic Signatures (QES), and maintain a complete audit trail on the matter record.

## Problem Statement
Legal practices struggle with fragmented communication channels and sluggish document execution. Relying on disconnected email threads, postal delays, and wet signatures adds days to matter completion, increases administrative overhead, and creates compliance gaps in the matter audit trail.

## Solution
PCMS integrates WhatsApp, SMS messaging, and end-to-end digital signing (including QES compliant with England, Wales, and Scotland legal standards) directly into matter workflows. Fee earners can send reminders, request documentation, and issue signing requests directly from the matter file, tracking delivery status, read receipts, and signature verification in real time.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp and SMS messaging from workflows | Clients receive updates on their preferred mobile channels | Faster document collection and response times |
| Qualified Electronic Signatures (QES) & e-signatures | Eliminate postal delays and wet-ink signature friction | Accelerated matter closure by 2–3 days |
| Automatic communication & signature logging to matter file | Complete audit trail for SRA and Law Society of Scotland compliance | Defensible compliance posture and full matter transparency |
| Pre-templated message & signing workflows | Standardize requests, prevent errors, and automate chase sequences | Recover 1+ hours per fee earner per day |
| Real-time delivery, read receipts, and signature tracking | Complete visibility into client engagement and document execution | Eliminate follow-up uncertainty and enhance client experience |
