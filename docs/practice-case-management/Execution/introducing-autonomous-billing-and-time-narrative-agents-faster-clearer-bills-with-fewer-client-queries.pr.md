# Billing and time-narrative agents

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** AI-Driven Billing & Time Narrative Agents
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Introducing Autonomous Billing and Time-Narrative Agents—Faster, Clearer Bills with Fewer Client Queries

## Summary
Legal practices now have access to their first autonomous agents for billing workflows. A time-narrative agent ensures time descriptions are accurate and clear, while an autonomous billing agent leverages those narratives to prepare higher-quality bills. The result: bills reach clients sooner and with significantly fewer follow-up inquiries, improving cash flow and client satisfaction.

## Problem Statement
Mid-market and large legal practices struggle with billing friction. Fee earners record time in fragmented formats with inconsistent descriptions, leading to unclear narratives that confuse clients and trigger expensive re-work queries. Manual bill preparation is time-consuming and error-prone, delaying cash flow and damaging client trust. Partners waste billable hours reviewing and correcting time entries and bill drafts instead of serving clients.

## Solution
Two new AI agents automate and enhance billing quality end-to-end. The time-narrative agent analyzes raw time entries, standardizes descriptions, and generates clear, client-ready narratives that reflect actual work performed. The autonomous billing agent ingests those polished narratives, applies firm rules and billing standards, and produces compliant, professional bills ready for delivery—eliminating manual rework and accelerating the billing cycle.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Time-Narrative Agent | Transforms fragmented time entries into clear, standardized narratives | Eliminates ambiguous descriptions; reduces client confusion and query volume |
| Autonomous Billing Agent | Generates compliant bills directly from enriched time narratives | Cuts manual bill preparation time by 60%; faster cash flow |
| Integrated Quality Validation | Validates bills against firm billing rules and regulatory standards before delivery | Ensures consistency and reduces costly errors and rework |

## Customer Benefits
- **Faster Cash Flow:** Bills are prepared and delivered days sooner, accelerating revenue recognition and improving working capital.
- **Fewer Client Queries:** Clear, professional time narratives eliminate confusion and drastically reduce client follow-up questions and disputes.
- **Recovered Billable Time:** Partners and fee earners reclaim hours previously spent on manual bill review and correction, redirecting effort to client work.
- **Improved Client Satisfaction:** Transparent, well-documented bills build trust and reduce friction in the billing conversation.
