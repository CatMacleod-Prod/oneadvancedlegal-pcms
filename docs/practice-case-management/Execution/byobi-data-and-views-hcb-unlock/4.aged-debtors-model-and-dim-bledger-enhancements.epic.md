# Aged Debtors Model and DIM_Bledger Enhancements

## Strategic Context
Monitoring aged receivables and invoice dispatch statuses is critical for managing firm cash flow and mitigating bad debt. The legacy practice management system calculates aging buckets via udf_AgedDebtors2 and lacks dimension-level tracking of printed/dispatched bill states in analytical models. Replicating AgedDebtors2 in DBT with configurable aging bands and enhancing DIM_Bledger with the BledgerPrinted attribute gives finance teams immediate insight into overdue invoices and billing lifecycle progression.

## Success Criteria
- Complete parity with legacy udf_AgedDebtors2 debt aging calculations across 30, 60, 90, and 120+ day buckets.
- Successful inclusion of `BledgerPrinted` flag and timestamp in `DIM_Bledger` across all historical and new billing records.
- Accelerated aged debtor report generation from minutes to sub-second responses in Snowflake.

## Scope

### In Scope
- DBT model and Snowflake view replacement for `udf_AgedDebtors2`.
- Extension of `DIM_Bledger` dimension model to incorporate `BledgerPrinted` tracking attributes.
- Configurable aging bucket calculations based on invoice date, due date, or cutoff date.
- Aggregation by matter, fee earner, client, and practice department.

### Out of Scope
- Automated debt collection or credit control email dispatch (handled by PCMS workflow engine).
- Adjusting nominal ledger bad-debt provisions.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN an aged debtors query is executed with a reference date, the Snowflake Aged Debtors Model SHALL categorize outstanding balances into 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-120, and 120+ day aging bands.

WHEN `DIM_Bledger` is queried for an invoice, the dimension model SHALL return the `BledgerPrinted` status indicator and initial print/dispatch timestamp.

WHERE an invoice has been partially settled prior to the cutoff date, the Aged Debtors Model SHALL calculate aging solely on the remaining unpaid balance.

IF a bill has been credited or written off prior to the reference date, THEN the Aged Debtors Model SHALL exclude the invoice from aged debtor totals.

## Business Rules
- Debt aging calculations must default to invoice dispatch date when `BledgerPrinted` is true, falling back to draft creation date otherwise.
- Aging buckets must dynamically compute relative to the user-supplied cutoff parameter, not current system date.
- Disputed bills must remain visible in aged debtor models with an active dispute flag.

## Dependencies
- IPS-574 aggregating transformation framework.
- Upstream billing ledger tables and print audit event logs synced to Snowflake.
