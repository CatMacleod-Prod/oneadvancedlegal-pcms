# Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules Engine

## Strategic Context
Scottish legal practices are governed by distinct Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules requiring strict validation on client account postings, disbursements, and overdraft prevention. Without automated enforcement, cashiering teams rely on manual checks, introducing regulatory compliance risk, audit failure exposure, and delayed financial postings. Implementing a native rules engine directly into PCMS enforces automated controls at the point of transaction entry, eliminating manual review overhead and ensuring compliance across Scottish operations.

## Success Criteria
- 100% of client transactions validated against Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules prior to ledger commit
- Zero undetected client account balance deficits or improper disbursement postings
- Reduction of manual transaction validation time by 75% for Scottish legal accounts

## Scope

### In Scope
- Real-time rule validation on all Scottish client ledger postings and transfers
- Automated prevention of client account overdraws and prohibited inter-ledger transfers
- Automated compliance alerts and blocking mechanisms for rule violations during posting

### Out of Scope
- SRA-specific English and Welsh rule validation (handled via Dual-Jurisdiction Compliance Markers epic)
- Automated banking reconciliation logic (handled via Automated Open Banking Cashier Reconciliation epic)

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a cashier submits a client ledger transaction for a Scottish matter, the Rules Engine SHALL validate the transaction against Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules before committing to the ledger.

IF a client account transfer would result in a negative ledger balance, THEN the Rules Engine SHALL block the posting and display a non-compliance alert specifying the breached rule.

WHEN a disbursement is recorded against a client ledger without cleared funds present, the Rules Engine SHALL flag the transaction as requiring partner authorisation.

WHERE a Scottish jurisdiction tag is active on a matter, the Rules Engine SHALL enforce mandatory 14-day client money reconciliation validation rules.

## Business Rules
- Scottish client account transactions cannot be posted retrospectively beyond the allowable regulatory grace window without senior partner override.
- Transactions flagged with rule breaches must log the attempting user, timestamp, rule identifier, and matter ID to the compliance audit log.

## Dependencies
- Core PCMS General Ledger service
- Matter jurisdiction metadata service
