# SLAB Accounting Audit Trail & Reporting

## Strategic Context
Firms handling Scottish Legal Aid Board (SLAB) funded matters are subject to stringent accounting audits and precise claim verification requirements. Inadequate audit trails and fragmented disbursement documentation result in rejected legal aid claims, delayed fee recoveries, and audit penalties. A dedicated SLAB accounting audit trail and reporting module provides immutable tracking of legal aid receipts, outlays, and fee payments, delivering instant audit readiness and zero claim rework.

## Success Criteria
- 100% audit-ready transaction traceability for all SLAB matter disbursements, claims, and receipts
- Zero rejected SLAB financial claims due to missing or non-compliant audit metadata
- Instant single-click generation of SLAB compliance audit packs

## Scope

### In Scope
- Tamper-proof transaction logging for all SLAB-related receipts, outlays, and transfers
- Standardized SLAB financial reporting exports and compliance audit pack generator
- Dedicated SLAB account balancing and fee recovery reconciliation views

### Out of Scope
- Direct electronic claim submission via SLAB EDI/API gateway (defer to Legal Aid Submissions epic)
- Legal aid eligibility assessment and case qualification forms

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a transaction is posted against a SLAB-designated matter, the Audit Trail Engine SHALL record an immutable entry containing cashier ID, timestamp, transaction reference, and SLAB claim reference.

WHERE a SLAB audit report is requested for a date range, the Reporting Module SHALL compile all disbursements, counsel fees, and client outlays into the official SLAB audit pack format.

IF an outlay is recorded without a mandatory SLAB outlay code or supporting receipt attachment, THEN the System SHALL prevent final transaction posting until mandatory metadata is supplied.

WHEN SLAB remittance payments are received, the System SHALL allocate payments against specific fee and outlay claims, highlighting any deductions or variances.

## Business Rules
- Audit trail logs for SLAB matters are immutable and cannot be edited, purged, or deleted by any user role.
- Outlays claimed under legal aid must strictly reference approved SLAB fee structure categories.
