# Heal Desk improvements

## Release Information

**Product/Module:** Heal Desk
**Version:** 8.0

## Headline

Targeted improvements to eliminate everyday friction with targeted usability improvements designed to keep practices running smoothly.

## Summary

A focused set of quality-of-life improvements focused on stabilising Heal Desk customers that remove common operational friction points encountered by daily users. These enhancements are not new paid features, but rather refinements to existing workflows that increase practitioner satisfaction and accelerate matter throughput. The release strengthens gross revenue retention by ensuring existing customers remain engaged and efficient.

## Problem Statement

Legal practitioners encounter small but cumulative friction points in everyday workflows—inefficient navigation patterns, redundant data entry steps, and micro-interactions that slow down routine tasks. While individual pain points may seem minor, they collectively consume unbillable time and erode user satisfaction. Practitioners expect their case management tools to work seamlessly without interruption, and even minor usability gaps can create frustration that impacts retention and loyalty.

## Solution

This workstream targets the most-reported usability friction points through surgical refinements to existing features. Improvements focus on streamlining navigation, reducing repetitive steps, and enhancing real-time feedback during common actions. These changes are delivered as standard enhancements to all Heal Desk customers at no additional cost, demonstrating commitment to customer success and continuous quality improvement.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features                                    | Key Benefits                                      | Value & Impact Delivered                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Streamlined navigation patterns                 | Faster access to frequently used functions        | Reduced daily clicks and navigation steps |
| Eliminated redundant data entry workflows       | Less time spent on administrative overhead        | Recovery of unbillable practitioner time  |
| Enhanced micro-interactions and visual feedback | Clearer confirmation of actions and state changes | Improved confidence and reduced errors    |

## Customer Quote (Imagined)

## Call to Action

Heal Desk customers can access Release 8 improvements immediately in their accounts. Review the in-app release notes for specific enhancements and best practices. Contact your account manager if you have questions about leveraging these improvements in your practice workflows.

## Internal Notes

Release 8 is a retention-focused quality improvement cycle. These improvements do not represent new billable features or revenue-generating capabilities; instead, they protect and strengthen gross revenue retention (GRR) by removing everyday friction that might otherwise encourage customer churn. The release emphasizes continuous incremental improvement as part of the quarterly enhancement cadence outlined in the Heal Desk UX vision, reinforcing user satisfaction and loyalty among existing mid-market and large UK legal practice customers.
