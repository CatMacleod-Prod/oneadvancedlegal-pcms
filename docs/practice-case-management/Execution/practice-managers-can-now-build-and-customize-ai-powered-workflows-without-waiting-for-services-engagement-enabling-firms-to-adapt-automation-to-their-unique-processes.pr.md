# Build your own AI workflows

## Release Information

**Product/Module:** Practice Case Management System (PCMS) – AI Workflow Builder
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline

Law Firms can now build and customize workflows powered by AI, enabling firms to adapt automation to their unique processes.

## Summary

We're releasing the AI Workflow Builder, empowering practice managers and partners to create, configure, and modify AI-driven workflows directly within PCMS without requiring custom development or external services. Firms can now shape how workflow automation fits their specific processes, adapt workflows as their needs change, and accelerate matter throughput without operational delays. This self-service capability reduces dependency on implementation services while maintaining enterprise-grade governance and compliance.

## Problem Statement

Mid-market and large UK legal practices face critical operational friction when implementing workflow automation. Currently, firms must engage expensive services teams to design and deploy custom AI workflows, creating bottlenecks when business requirements shift. Practice managers lack direct control over workflow logic, forcing them to choose between accepting rigid pre-built automations or incurring lengthy—and costly—re-engagement cycles. This dependency slows adaptation, constrains profitability, and prevents firms from optimizing workflows to match their unique matter types, fee structures, and jurisdictional requirements.

## Solution

The AI Workflow Builder provides firms with an intuitive, no-code interface to design and deploy automations directly within PCMS. Firms can now define trigger conditions, route decisions, approval gateways, and integration endpoints without coding expertise.&#x20;
