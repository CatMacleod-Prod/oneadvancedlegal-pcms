# Document History Persistence & SQM Limit Integration

## Strategic Context
Compliance and audit integrity require complete version control of all generated claim artefacts alongside firm-wide oversight of Specialist Quality Mark (SQM) certificate limits. Automatically persisting generated Claim 1A XML and Legal Forms documents to Document History, while tracking Total Work vs Certificate Limits, guarantees full regulatory compliance and prevents billing overruns.

## Success Criteria
- 100% of generated XML files and Legal Forms Claim 1A documents versioned in Document History.
- Real-time visibility into Total Work vs Certificate Limits for Family Legal Aid matters.
- Full auditability of claim generation, retries, and regenerations with recorded timestamps and user identities.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Automated storage and versioning of Claim 1A XML files and Legal Forms artefacts in Document History.
- Version tagging and status tracking (`Generating`, `Successful`, `Failed`, `Regenerated`).
- SQM Total Work vs Certificate Limit tracking and threshold alerts (linked to PRU-3316).
- Audit trail logging for generation attempts, failure diagnostics, and user-initiated regenerations.

### Out of Scope
- General SQM reporting across non-legal aid matter types.
- Permanent manual deletion of generated statutory claim artefacts.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN Claim 1A XML and Legal Forms documents are generated, the Document History Engine SHALL persist both artefacts as linked, version-controlled records on the matter.

WHERE a claim is regenerated following data corrections, the Document History Engine SHALL create a new version record while preserving historical claim artefacts in the audit log.

WHEN a claim generation process completes or fails, the System SHALL record an immutable audit event containing user identity, timestamp, outcome status, and diagnostic details.

WHILE viewing a Family Fixed Fee matter, the System SHALL display the current SQM Total Work value alongside the active Certificate Limit with visual warning thresholds when approaching limits.

## Business Rules
- Claim artefacts cannot be permanently purged from Document History once generated, in accordance with SRA and LAA retention rules.
- Regeneration of claim files requires the user to input a mandatory reason, which is appended to the matter audit trail.

## Dependencies
- Document Management & Integration Service.
- PCMS Core Legal Accounts & Regulatory Compliance Engine.
