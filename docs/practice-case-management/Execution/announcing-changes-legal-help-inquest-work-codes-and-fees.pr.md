# Announcing changes Legal Help Inquest Work codes and fees

## Headline

NOT YET CONFIRMED - PLACEHOLDER ONLY - Comprehensive Legal Help Inquest Fee Scheme Updates and Universal Legal Aid XML Schema Support for Compliant Claims Submissions

## Summary

We are introducing end-to-end workflow, remuneration, and validation updates across the Practice Case Management System to support the Ministry of Justice Legal Help Inquest reforms and the Public Authority (Hillsborough Law) accountability framework. Crucially, this release updates the core Legal Aid XML export schema to the new LAA structural standards, ensuring seamless digital claim generation and zero-rejection submissions across all Legal Aid disciplines ahead of the January submission cycle.

## Problem Statement

Forthcoming MoJ reforms to inquest legal aid and the Public Authority Accountability Bill introduce unified inquest fee structures, revised remuneration rules, and expanded legal aid availability for bereaved families. Concurrently, the Legal Aid Agency is introducing mandatory structural revisions to the universal Legal Aid bulk XML claim submission schema. Without automated system-level adjustments, legal practices face high risks of claim rejections, non-compliance, manual billing workarounds, and complete disruption to their monthly Legal Aid Agency claim submissions across all categories of law.

## Solution

This release embeds updated Legal Help Inquest fee structures, automated remuneration calculations, and revised regulatory validations directly into matter intake and billing workflows. In addition, the export engine has been rebuilt to generate the new LAA-compliant XML file structure across all civil and legal aid claim submissions. Pre-submission schema validation detects missing data or formatting errors prior to export, safeguarding cash flow and ensuring continuous, automated compliance with updated LAA standards.
