# Purchase Ledger Approval Routing and Invoice Attachments

## Strategic Context
Mid-market and large law firms handle hundreds of supplier invoices, counsel fee notes, and office disbursements monthly. Manual, paper-based authorization and unstructured email sign-offs lead to administrative delays, misplaced invoices, and risk of unauthorized firm expenditure. This epic delivers configurable, multi-tier Purchase Ledger approval routing, spend threshold governance, and side-by-side digital invoice document verification directly within cashier workflows.

## Success Criteria
- 65%+ acceleration in supplier invoice approval cycle times.
- 100% compliance with firm spend authorization limits and role-based approval thresholds.
- Complete audit trail transparency for all supplier invoice approval decisions, rejections, and delegations.
- Zero posting of unapproved invoices to the Purchase Ledger or Nominal accounts.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Multi-tier invoice authorization rules based on nominal ledger codes, department/practice groups, and financial spend thresholds.
- Digital invoice attachment drawer supporting PDF and image previews rendered side-by-side with ledger entry data.
- Structured approval queue for designated approvers (Fee Earners, Department Heads, COFA/Partners) with approve, reject, and reassign actions.
- Mandatory rejection notes and notification alerts sent back to invoice submitters.
- Immutable audit log capturing approver identity, decision timestamps, spend threshold checks, and notes.

### Out of Scope
- Automated OCR/AI invoice data extraction.
- External vendor self-service billing portals.
- Client-to-Office matter fund transfers and bank reconciliations (handled in separate modules).

## Acceptance Criteria
- WHEN a supplier invoice is recorded in the Purchase Ledger, the routing engine SHALL assign approval tasks according to the configured financial threshold and department hierarchy.
- WHILE an approver reviews a pending invoice, the interface SHALL display the supplier invoice PDF in a side-by-side inspection viewer alongside line-item nominal allocations.
- WHEN an invoice receives final digital authorization from all required approvers, the accounts system SHALL update the invoice status to 'Approved' and allow posting to the Purchase Ledger.
- IF an approver rejects a submitted invoice, THEN the system SHALL mandate an explanatory rejection reason and notify the originating cashier or fee earner.
- The system SHALL log an immutable audit trail entry recording user ID, timestamp, approval tier, and comments for every authorization event.

## Business Rules
- Invoices up to £1,000 require single-tier sign-off by the originating Department Head.
- Invoices between £1,000 and £5,000 require secondary sign-off by the Finance Manager.
- Invoices exceeding £5,000 mandate dual-tier authorization including Partner / COFA sign-off.
- Invoices cannot be posted to the Purchase Ledger or released for payment without complete authorized status.

## Dependencies
- Document Management & Integration Service for invoice binary storage and PDF preview rendering.
- PCMS Core Legal Accounts module for supplier account and nominal ledger mappings.

## Open Questions
- Notification preferences (email digests vs real-time alerts) for partners with pending high-value invoice approvals.
