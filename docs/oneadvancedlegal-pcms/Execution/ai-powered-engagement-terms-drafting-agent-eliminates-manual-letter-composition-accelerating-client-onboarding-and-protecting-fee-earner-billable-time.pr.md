# Engagement terms drafting agent

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** PCMS Core Application – Document Management & Integration Service
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
AI-powered engagement terms drafting agent eliminates manual letter composition, accelerating client onboarding and protecting fee-earner billable time.

## Summary
The Engagement Terms Drafting Agent is an AI-driven capability that automatically prepares draft engagement letters by synthesizing client and matter data already held within the practice management system. Fee earners review and refine the draft—focusing only on judgement-driven customizations—before sending to clients, reducing composition time by up to 80% and accelerating matter onboarding cycles.

## Problem Statement
Fee earners currently spend 1–2 hours per new matter drafting engagement terms letters from scratch, manually extracting client details, matter scope, and standard terms from disparate sources or memory. This non-billable overhead delays client communication, slows matter velocity, and diverts experienced practitioners from higher-value legal work. Delays in sending engagement terms also create friction in the client onboarding experience and increase the risk of scope misalignment.

## Solution
The Engagement Terms Drafting Agent automatically generates a complete, practice-compliant engagement letter draft by drawing on client profile data, matter classification, jurisdiction flags, fee structure templates, and regulatory obligations already captured in PCMS. The agent produces a professionally formatted, legally sound starting point tailored to the specific matter context. Fee earners then spend their time only on matter-specific judgement calls—custom scope notes, special terms, or bespoke arrangements—rather than routine composition, before approving the letter for dispatch.
