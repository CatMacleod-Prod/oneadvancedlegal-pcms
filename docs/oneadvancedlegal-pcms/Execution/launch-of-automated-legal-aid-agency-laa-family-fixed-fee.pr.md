# Launch of automated Legal Aid Agency (LAA) Family Fixed Fee

## Headline
Practice & Case Management Launches End-to-End LAA Family Fixed Fee Claim 1A Generation and Digital Validation Engine

## Summary
Practice & Case Management introduces integrated Family Fixed Fee Claim 1A processing, eliminating legacy CUE modules and outdated Laserforms in favor of direct, in-system validation and digital document assembly. Legal aid practitioners can now validate matter claim data in real time, automatically generate portal-compliant Claim 1A XML files, and deep-link directly into modern Legal Forms for immediate review and filing. This release automates statutory validation, aligns with Specialist Quality Mark (SQM) certificate limits, and persists all generated claim artefacts directly into Document History for audit-ready compliance.

## Problem Statement
Family legal aid practitioners face administrative drag and submission rejection risks when preparing Legal Aid Agency (LAA) Family Fixed Fee claims. Legacy workflows rely on disconnected tools—including outdated CUE modules and legacy Laserform Claim 1A templates—that require manual data re-keying, complex multi-stage XML exports, and fragmented external error checking to meet LAA portal standards. These disjointed processes introduce data inaccuracies, fail to provide unified SQM certificate limit oversight, and lack resilient recovery mechanisms when document generation fails.

## Solution
The modernized Family Fixed Fee engine embeds complete Claim 1A preparation, validation, and generation directly into Practice & Case Management. Fee earners capture mandatory claim details within the matter workspace, trigger comprehensive business-rule validation with actionable error resolution, and generate fully compliant LAA Claim 1A XML payloads alongside editable Legal Forms documents. All claim artefacts are automatically version-controlled within Document History with transparent status tracking, failure recovery, and SQM certificate limit safeguards.
