# Press Release

## Headline

PCMS Announces 100-Day Customer Quality of Life Enhancement Program Featuring Dedicated Probate Financial Reporting

We are launching a dedicated 100-day initiative focused on delivering targeted quality-of-life enhancements and specialized reporting capabilities across the Practice Case Management System. This program prioritizes resolving key customer operational pain points, eliminating unbillable manual workarounds, and delivering dual-jurisdiction probate estate financial reporting across England, Wales, and Scotland.

## Problem Statement

Customers experience various friction points and usability challenges across day-to-day operations that drive helpdesk volume and impact user satisfaction. One such friction point is that probate practitioners managing multiple estates lack specialized reporting visibility into estate financial activity, forcing reliance on manual workarounds that consume significant unbillable time (2+ hours per case) and introduce error risks. The absence of dedicated probate reporting for assets, liabilities, income, and interim accounts delays decision-making, impairs workload management, and creates compliance exposure under SRA and Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules, impeding smooth legacy migrations from ALB and P4W.

## Solution

The 100-Day Quality of Life Enhancement program establishes a focused delivery cycle to implement highest-impact usability improvements, stability fixes, and high-value workflow enhancements. As a cornerstone enhancement, PCMS introduces a specialized Probate Estate Financial Reporting suite providing four core report types—Assets & Liabilities, Income Details, Appropriations, and Interim Accounts—integrated directly with the PCMS GL and enforcing dual-jurisdiction compliance across SRA and Law Society of Scotland standards. Additional quality-of-life improvements will be prioritized and delivered iteratively across the 100-day window based on user feedback and support metrics.
