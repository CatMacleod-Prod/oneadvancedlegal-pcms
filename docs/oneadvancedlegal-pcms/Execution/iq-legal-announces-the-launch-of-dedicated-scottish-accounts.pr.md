# IQ Legal announces the launch of dedicated Scottish Accounts

## Headline
IQ Legal launches Scottish Accounts Requirements and Law Society of Scotland compliance capabilities within Practice & Case Management for Q4 FY27

## Summary
IQ Legal is releasing dedicated Scottish Accounts Requirements and Law Society of Scotland compliance capabilities within Practice & Case Management (PCMS) in Q4 FY27. This release delivers automated enforcement of Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules, segregated Invested Client Funds (ICF) ledger handling, and dual-jurisdiction compliance markers for firms operating across SRA and Scottish regulatory frameworks. The update includes automated Open Banking cashier reconciliations and Scottish Legal Aid Board (SLAB) accounting audit trails to streamline compliance and reduce manual reconciliation overhead.

## Problem Statement
Scottish legal practices face complex dual-jurisdiction compliance requirements across Law Society of Scotland and SRA frameworks, with distinct Accounts Rules, ICF segregation standards, and SLAB audit trail obligations. Manual enforcement of Scottish-specific accounting rules, combined with fragmented reconciliation processes across Open Banking systems and legacy accounting workflows, creates operational friction and heightened compliance risk for multi-jurisdictional firms and Scottish-based practices.

## Solution
Practice & Case Management now integrates dedicated Scottish Accounts Requirements compliance automation, including real-time enforcement of Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules, native segregated ICF ledger handling aligned to Scottish standards, and dual-jurisdiction compliance markers that flag SRA vs. Scottish regulatory obligations. Automated Open Banking cashier reconciliations eliminate manual matching workflows, while SLAB accounting audit trails provide tamper-proof transaction records for audit and regulatory review. Firms can now manage Scottish and cross-border compliance within a single PCMS interface.
