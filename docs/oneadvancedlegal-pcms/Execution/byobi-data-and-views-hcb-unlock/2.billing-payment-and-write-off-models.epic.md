# Billing, Payment and Write-Off Models

## Strategic Context
Accurate financial reporting on billed revenue, client fee payments, and write-offs is essential for law firm partners and legal cashiers assessing matter realization and department revenue lockup. Historically, billing realization queries have depended on legacy SQL Server UDFs (udf_BillsByMatter, udf_PaymentsByMatter, udf_BillWosByMatter), preventing direct integration with firm BI platforms like Tableau or Power BI. Rebuilding these calculations within DBT and Snowflake enables high-performance billing analytics and auditable revenue tracking without manual reporting extracts.

## Success Criteria
- Exact numerical parity for billed fees, disbursements, VAT, received payments, and write-offs compared to legacy UDF outputs.
- Elimination of custom extract requests for practice-level billing and cash collection reports.
- Comprehensive date-cutoff capability enabling period-end lockup and realization reconciliations.

## Scope

### In Scope
- DBT model and Snowflake view replacement for `udf_BillsByMatter` (billed fees, disbursements, and VAT breakdown).
- DBT model and Snowflake view replacement for `udf_PaymentsByMatter` (payment allocations, receipts, and client credit balances).
- DBT model and Snowflake view replacement for `udf_BillWosByMatter` (bill write-offs, credit notes, and fee reductions).
- Multi-currency and VAT ledger tax handling within all billing transformations.

### Out of Scope
- Processing write-off transactions or bill approvals (remains within PCMS Core).
- Direct integration with external third-party general ledgers outside of Snowflake analytics.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a billing summary query is executed for a matter, the Snowflake Billing Model SHALL return total billed fees, billed disbursements, and VAT broken down by invoice and matter.

WHEN payment transactions are queried with a specified cutoff date, the Snowflake Payment Model SHALL calculate total cash receipts allocated to invoices on or before that date.

WHERE a bill contains full or partial write-offs or credit notes, the Bill Write-Off Model SHALL subtract write-off amounts to compute net realizable revenue.

IF a matter contains multi-rate VAT billing entries, THEN the Billing Model SHALL preserve individual tax band allocations per matter invoice.

## Business Rules
- VAT allocations must calculate based on transaction tax point dates rather than payment receipt dates.
- Write-offs must distinguish between unbilled WIP write-offs and post-billing invoice credit adjustments.
- Payment allocations must follow FIFO invoice settlement rules unless explicit bill allocations are recorded.

## Dependencies
- IPS-574 transformation capabilities for parameterised billing aggregations.
- Replicated billing, receipt, and credit note transactional tables available in Snowflake.

## Open Questions
