# Client and Disbursement Ledger Financial Models

## Strategic Context
Compliance with Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) Accounts Rules and Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules requires absolute segregation and auditable tracking of client funds and disbursement ledgers. Legacy implementations utilize SQL Server UDFs (udf_CledgerByMatter and udf_DledgerByMatter) that restrict financial reporting access and limit automated cashier reconciliation workflows. Migrating these core ledger views to DBT models in Snowflake provides transparent, auditable ledger histories and supports automated cashier compliance queries.

## Success Criteria
- 100% calculation parity with legacy client and disbursement ledger UDFs.
- Instant access to historical client ledger balances as of any historical cutoff date.
- Complete regulatory audit trail visibility across client funds and disbursement movements.

## Scope

### In Scope
- DBT model and Snowflake view replacement for `udf_CledgerByMatter` (client money receipts, payments, and balances).
- DBT model and Snowflake view replacement for `udf_DledgerByMatter` (matter disbursement ledger and recovery balances).
- Point-in-time balance reconstruction using date-cutoff filtering.
- Dual-jurisdiction compliance markers for England & Wales (SRA) and Scotland (Law Society of Scotland).

### Out of Scope
- Execution of client-to-office money transfers (managed by PCMS Core approval engine).
- Direct Open Banking transaction matching execution (handled in PCMS Cashroom).

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a client ledger query is run with a historical date filter, the Snowflake Cledger Model SHALL compute the exact cleared client balance for the matter as of that timestamp.

WHEN a disbursement ledger query executes, the Snowflake Dledger Model SHALL output all billed and unbilled disbursement line items, VAT components, and payment statuses.

WHERE invested client funds or interest bearing accounts are configured, the Cledger Model SHALL include designated interest accruals in the total client ledger balance.

IF a matter ledger reflects an overdrawn client balance condition, THEN the Cledger Model SHALL surface an audit exception flag for compliance review.

## Business Rules
- Client money balances must never aggregate across distinct legal entities unless formal multi-party matter agreements exist.
- Scottish Invested Client Funds (ICF) must remain segregated in ledger views while rolling up to overall matter balances.
- All ledger debit/credit postings must maintain double-entry balance validation.

## Dependencies
- IPS-574 support for parameterized recursive ledger balance calculation.
- Synchronized raw ledger posting tables in Snowflake.
