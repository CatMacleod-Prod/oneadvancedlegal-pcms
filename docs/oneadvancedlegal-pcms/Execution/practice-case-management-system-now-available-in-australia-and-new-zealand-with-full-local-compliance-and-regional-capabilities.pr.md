# Launch in Australia and New Zealand

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Practice Case Management System (PCMS)
**Version:** ANZ Regional Release – Q4 FY26

## Headline
Practice Case Management System Now Available in Australia and New Zealand with Full Local Compliance and Regional Capabilities

## Summary
Legal practices in Australia and New Zealand can now access the Practice Case Management System for the first time, with purpose-built local capabilities tailored to their jurisdictional requirements. This regional expansion delivers enterprise-grade case management, practice accounting, and document automation tools designed specifically for mid-market and large legal firms across the ANZ region. The launch opens a significant new market for the platform and enables Australian and New Zealand law firms to operate with the same operational efficiency and regulatory compliance assurance as their UK counterparts.

## Problem Statement
Australian and New Zealand law firms struggle with fragmented legacy systems, unstable desktop integrations, and manual workflows that consume countless unbillable hours. Practices lack a unified, cloud-native platform that consolidates case management, legal accounting, and document handling while maintaining strict compliance with local regulatory frameworks (Australian Legal Services Board / Law Council of Australia, New Zealand Law Society). Without an integrated system built for their jurisdiction, mid-market and large firms cannot maximize fee-earner utilization, accelerate matter throughput, or confidently manage multi-office operations at scale.

## Solution
The Practice Case Management System delivers a purpose-built enterprise SaaS platform engineered for Australian and New Zealand legal practices. The release includes localized legal accounting modules validated for ANZ regulatory standards, seamless Microsoft Outlook integration (Classic and New Outlook), automated practice workflows tailored to common ANZ practice areas (conveyancing, litigation, family, probate, and corporate), digital client onboarding with local identity verification, and real-time Open Banking reconciliation. The system processes up to 500 cases per day per firm with sub-200ms latency, scales to 100+ concurrent users, and provides automated audit trails and compliance reporting built directly into the platform's core operations.
