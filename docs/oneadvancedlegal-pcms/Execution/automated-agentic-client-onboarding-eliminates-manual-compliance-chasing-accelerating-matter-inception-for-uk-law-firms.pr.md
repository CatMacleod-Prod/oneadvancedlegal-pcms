# Agentic client onboarding&#x20;

## Release Information

**Product/Module:** PCMS Core Application – Client & Matter Inception
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline

Automated agentic client onboarding eliminates manual compliance chasing, accelerating matter inception for UK law firms.

## Summary

Law firms can now onboard new clients with significantly reduced manual administrative overhead through automated agentic workflows that orchestrate identity verification, anti-money-laundering (AML), and conflict-of-interest checks as a seamless part of the onboarding process. Integrated third-party KYC/ID/AML providers (Thirdfort, Minerva, Legl) execute these compliance checks automatically, allowing fee earners to focus billable time on client relationship building rather than compliance administration, and enabling matters to open faster.

## Problem Statement

Mid-market and large UK law practices currently waste substantial fee-earner capacity on non-billable client onboarding administration. Manual identity verification, AML screening, and conflict checking create operational friction, delay matter inception, and prevent fee earners from deploying their expertise where it generates revenue. Fragmented third-party compliance point solutions require manual data entry and follow-up chasing, compounding delays and introducing compliance risk through human error.

## Solution

The PCMS Client & Matter Inception workflow launched in FY27 Q3, now features fully automated agentic workflows that orchestrate identity verification, AML screening, and conflict-of-interest checking as integrated stages within the digital client onboarding journey. Intelligent agents seamlessly coordinate with embedded third-party KYC/ID/AML providers, eliminating manual chasing and data re-entry. Fee earners receive real-time compliance status visibility and automated escalation only when human intervention is required, allowing them to remain focused on client engagement while compliance runs in parallel.
