# Probate Reports Suite Implementation

## Strategic Context
Probate practitioners managing multiple estates currently lack specialized reporting visibility into estate financial activity, forcing reliance on manual workarounds that consume significant unbillable time and introduce errors. The absence of probate-specific reports for assets, liabilities, income, and interim accounts creates operational friction that delays decision-making, impairs workload management, and risks compliance gaps with SRA and Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules. Delivering this epic directly enables the ALB and P4W customer migration while strengthening competitive positioning in the probate market and demonstrating clear ROI through time savings and accuracy gains.

## Success Criteria
- All four report types (Assets & Liabilities, Income Details, Appropriations, Interim Accounts) are live and accessible within the PCMS interface
- Probate firms report a measurable reduction in manual reporting time (target: 2+ hours saved per case)
- Compliance with dual-jurisdiction accounting rules (SRA and Law Society of Scotland) is validated and certified
- Customer adoption rate reaches 80% among ALB and P4W migrating customers within 30 days of release

## Scope

### In Scope
- Assets & Liabilities report showing estate composition and financial position at any administration stage
- Income Details report tracking all estate income received, sources, and distribution to beneficiaries
- Appropriations report documenting all distributions made to beneficiaries with supporting detail
- Interim Accounts report providing periodic estate accounting snapshots reconciling assets, liabilities, income, and disbursements
- Integration with PCMS accounting data model and GL structure
- Dual-jurisdiction compliance enforcement (SRA and Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules)
- Report generation, export, and drill-down capabilities
- Audit trail and compliance documentation features

### Out of Scope
- Custom report builder or ad-hoc query tool (defer to future reporting extensibility epic)
- Real-time data streaming or webhooks (defer to reporting infrastructure roadmap)
- Integration with third-party probate platforms outside PCMS ecosystem
- Multi-currency or international estate accounting (scope limited to GBP and UK jurisdictions)

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a probate user navigates to the Reports section in PCMS, the system SHALL display all four report types (Assets & Liabilities, Income Details, Appropriations, Interim Accounts) as distinct, selectable options with clear descriptions of each report's purpose.

WHEN a user generates an Assets & Liabilities report, the report SHALL display the complete estate composition including all asset accounts, liability accounts, and net position, reconciled to the GL at the selected reporting date.

WHEN a user generates an Income Details report, the report SHALL display all estate income grouped by source, tax status, and distribution allocations to beneficiaries for the selected period.

WHEN a user generates an Appropriations report, the report SHALL display all capital and income asset distributions made to beneficiaries with transfer dates, asset valuations, and residual balances.

WHEN a user generates an Interim Accounts report, the report SHALL reconcile all assets, liabilities, income, and disbursements into a unified balance sheet and income statement snapshot compliant with estate accounting standards.

WHERE dual-jurisdiction rules apply, the system SHALL enforce jurisdiction-specific accounting formats (SRA Accounts Rules for England & Wales, Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules for Scotland) based on the matter's jurisdiction setting.

IF unposted general ledger transactions or unallocated cash entries exist for an estate, THEN the report generator SHALL display a reconciliation warning banner identifying the unposted items.

## Business Rules
- Estate valuations and ledger balances must reconcile to the General Ledger as of 23:59:59 on the selected report cut-off date.
- Dual-jurisdiction rules require separate client and executry ledger handling under Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules versus SRA Accounts Rules.
- All report generations, exports (PDF/Excel), and parameter changes must be recorded in the matter audit log with timestamp and user ID.
- Access to probate reports is restricted to users with assigned Probate Practitioner, Cashier, or Firm Administrator roles.

## Dependencies
- PCMS Core Accounting Data Model and General Ledger service.
- Dual-jurisdiction compliance rule engine (SRA and Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules).
- Matter audit logging service for tracking reporting operations.
- PDF and spreadsheet export rendering pipeline.
