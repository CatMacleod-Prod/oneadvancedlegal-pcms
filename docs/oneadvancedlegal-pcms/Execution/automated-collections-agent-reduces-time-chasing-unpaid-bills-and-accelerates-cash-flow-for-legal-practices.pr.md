# Collections and cash agent

## Headline
Automated Collections Agent Reduces Time Chasing Unpaid Bills and Accelerates Cash Flow for Legal Practices and Client Debt Recovery

## Summary
The Collections and Cash Agent extends the cash collection workflow with intelligent automation that systematically follows up on unpaid bills without manual intervention. Legal practices recover cash faster and redirect fee-earner time from administrative chasing to billable work, while establishing embedded integration into the Intelligent Debt module for firms conducting debt recovery on behalf of their clients.

## Problem Statement
Mid-market and large UK legal practices spend significant unbillable time manually tracking, chasing, and following up on outstanding invoices across multiple matters. Fee earners are diverted from revenue-generating work to send reminder emails, review aging reports, and coordinate with cashiers on collection status. This administrative overhead delays cash recovery and strains practice cash flow. Furthermore, practice debt recovery and dispute teams lack automated, high-volume collection workflows when pursuing debt recovery on behalf of firm clients.

## Solution
The Collections and Cash Agent automates the entire follow-up workflow by monitoring unpaid bills, triggering intelligent reminder sequences at configurable intervals, and escalating overdue accounts based on practice-defined rules. The agent integrates with the PCMS cash collection module, leverages live matter and client records, and coordinates with Practice Management workflows to ensure consistent, compliant collection activity. Additionally, the agent's core engine is architected to embed directly into the Intelligent Debt module, empowering legal practices to run automated, multi-channel recovery workflows on client debt portfolios.
