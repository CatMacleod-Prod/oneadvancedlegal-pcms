# Legal Forms Claim 1A Document Integration

## Strategic Context
Legacy claim preparation relied on outdated Laserform templates that lacked dynamic cloud integration. Modernizing the workflow to generate dynamic Claim 1A forms within modern Legal Forms enables real-time field population, seamless browser-based editing, and integrated document review prior to filing.

## Success Criteria
- Automated, error-free mapping of matter claim metadata into Legal Forms Claim 1A templates.
- 1-click deep-link navigation directly into Legal Forms in Edit mode.
- Complete replacement and decommissioning of legacy Laserform templates.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Legal Forms Claim 1A template binding and automated metadata pre-population.
- Deep-link generation pointing to the compiled Claim 1A form in Edit mode.
- Bi-directional sync between PCMS matter claim data and Legal Forms fields.
- Document generation error handling and isolated retry workflows.

### Out of Scope
- Legacy Laserform format backward compatibility.
- Direct physical print spooling from PCMS.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN claim validation is successful, the Legal Forms Integration Service SHALL generate a dynamic Claim 1A document pre-populated with captured matter claim data.

WHEN document generation succeeds, the System SHALL present a deep-link action labeled 'Edit in Legal Forms'.

WHEN the user clicks the 'Edit in Legal Forms' deep link, the System SHALL open the generated Claim 1A document in Edit mode within Legal Forms in a new tab.

IF Legal Forms document generation fails, THEN the System SHALL preserve matter state, log the service error, and provide a single-click retry action.

## Business Rules
- User authentication and permissions must seamlessly pass through the deep link via Entra ID / OneAdvanced Identity Platform.
- Any subsequent edits made within Legal Forms must sync back to the stored document artefact in PCMS.

## Dependencies
- Modern Legal Forms API Service.
- LAA Business Rules & Validation Engine.
