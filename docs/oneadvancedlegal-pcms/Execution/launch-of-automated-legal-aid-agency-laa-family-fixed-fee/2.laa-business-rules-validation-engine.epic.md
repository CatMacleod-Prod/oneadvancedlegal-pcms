# LAA Business Rules & Validation Engine

## Strategic Context
Firms preparing Family Legal Aid claims previously relied on the legacy CUE module to perform data validation before submitting to the LAA portal. Embedding an in-system LAA business rules engine into PCMS eliminates manual multi-step validation checks, provides fee earners with instant inline remediation feedback, and guarantees first-time portal acceptance.

## Success Criteria
- Zero schema or business rule rejection errors upon portal upload.
- Comprehensive pre-submission validation executed with sub-second response times.
- Clear, actionable inline error guidance for every validation breach.

## Scope

### In Scope
- In-system validation engine replicating and modernizing all legacy CUE business rules.
- Pre-submission field validation, dependency checking, and cross-field integrity rules.
- Inline error reporting modal with actionable direct-link remediation guidance.
- Atomic validation state handling prior to artefact generation.

### Out of Scope
- Generation of physical XML payloads or Legal Forms documents.
- Portal-side claim approval or fee remittance processing.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN the fee earner selects Submit on a completed claim form, the Validation Engine SHALL evaluate all captured data against statutory LAA business rules.

IF any business rule or mandatory dependency is breached, THEN the Validation Engine SHALL block artefact generation and display an itemized error summary with direct field links.

WHEN the fee earner corrects identified validation errors and re-triggers Submit, the Validation Engine SHALL re-evaluate the claim dataset.

WHERE all statutory business rules and data constraints are satisfied, the Validation Engine SHALL transition the claim state to Approved for Generation.

## Business Rules
- Cross-field rules must enforce date alignment (e.g., Matter Start Date cannot be after Case Conclusion Date).
- Level of Work tariffs and statutory uplift percentages must match current LAA Family Fixed Fee schedules.

## Dependencies
- Family Fixed Fee Matter Configuration & Claim Data Capture epic.
- Document Management & Integration Service.
