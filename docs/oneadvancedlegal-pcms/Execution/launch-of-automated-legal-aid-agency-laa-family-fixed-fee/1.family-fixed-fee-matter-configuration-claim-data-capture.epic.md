# Family Fixed Fee Matter Configuration & Claim Data Capture

## Strategic Context
Legal aid practitioners currently face administrative friction and fragmented matter setup when preparing Legal Aid Agency (LAA) Family Fixed Fee claims. Capturing mandatory statutory claim parameters directly within the core matter workspace ensures clean data entry, reduces downstream submission defects, and establishes an integrated baseline for automated Claim 1A validation and generation.

## Success Criteria
- 100% of required LAA Family Fixed Fee claim fields are capturable within the matter workspace.
- Matter fee earners can assign the Family Fixed Fee module and toggle claim readiness without data loss.
- Form submission action is dynamically enabled only when all mandatory statutory fields are populated.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Assignment and removal of the Family Fixed Fee module on legal matters.
- Data capture screens for all mandatory and optional LAA Claim 1A fields.
- Dynamic form state management and submission action enablement.
- Matter-level autosave and draft claim persistence.

### Out of Scope
- Direct XML serialization and export (handled in Claim 1A XML Payload Generation & Export epic).
- External submission gateway transmission to the LAA portal.

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a fee earner opens an active family law matter, the Matter Workspace SHALL provide an option to assign the Family Fixed Fee module.

WHERE the Family Fixed Fee module is assigned, the Claim Capture Form SHALL render all mandatory statutory claim fields and applicable optional parameters.

WHILE any mandatory claim field remains empty or invalid, the Claim Form SHALL disable the Submit action and display field-level completion indicators.

WHEN all mandatory claim fields contain valid input, the Claim Form SHALL dynamically enable the Submit action.

## Business Rules
- Mandatory claim fields include LAA Certificate Number, Level of Work, Case Stage, Fee Scheme Code, Matter Start Date, and Lead Fee Earner.
- Modifying matter details while a claim is in Draft status automatically triggers draft state recalculation.

## Dependencies
- PCMS Core Matter & Practice Workflow Engine.
- LAA Business Rules & Validation Engine.
