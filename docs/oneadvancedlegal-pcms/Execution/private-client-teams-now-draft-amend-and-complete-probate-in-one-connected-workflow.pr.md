# 3

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Private Client Workflow
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Private Client Teams Now Draft, Amend, and Complete Probate in One Connected Workflow

## Summary
Private client teams can now draft wills, amend wills, and handle probate within a single integrated workflow powered by Scribe AI drafting and National Will Register connectivity. By eliminating manual re-keying and automating registration searches, teams reduce administrative overhead and registration risk while accelerating will work completion.

## Problem Statement
Private client practices currently manage will drafting, amendments, and probate across disconnected systems and manual processes. Fee earners re-key information across multiple platforms, critical will registrations are frequently missed or delayed, and the lack of automation between drafting and registration creates operational friction that extends matter timelines and increases compliance risk.

## Solution
The Private Client Workflow integrates will drafting with AI-assisted Scribe capabilities, direct connectivity to the National Will Register for registration and searching, and streamlined probate handling in a single connected interface. Teams draft documents once, automatically populate registration data, and eliminate manual re-keying, creating a fail-safe, end-to-end will lifecycle within PCMS.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Scribe AI-Assisted Will Drafting | Accelerate initial will creation with intelligent template population and clause suggestions | Reduce drafting time per will by up to 40% |
| National Will Register Integration | Automatically search existing registrations and submit new registrations without manual form completion | Eliminate missed registrations; reduce administrative overhead by 2+ hours per matter |
| Connected Will Amendment Workflow | Track and manage will amendments alongside original documents within a single matter record | Improve audit trail clarity and reduce version control errors |
| Probate Case Management | Handle probate progression, document requests, and estate accounting without switching platforms | Accelerate probate completion; improve fee earner utilization |
| Automated Data Population | Eliminate re-keying of client, executor, and asset information across drafting, registration, and probate stages | Reduce data entry errors and cut unbillable administrative time per matter |
