# Press Release

## Headline

PCMS, Time Capture, and Legal Forms Transition to Scalable Microservices Architecture and Adopt IQ Agentic Framework

## Summary

Wave 1 - Practice Case Management System (PCMS), Time Capture, and Legal Forms are evolving from existing cloud applications into a decoupled, high-performance microservices-based architecture. This architectural modernization integrates the IQ agentic framework across all three products, unlocking Microsoft 365 Model Context Protocol (MCP) capabilities to deliver intelligent AI overlays directly within Outlook and Word for UK legal practices.

## Problem Statement

While PCMS, Time Capture, and Legal Forms currently operate in the cloud, tightly coupled architectures and shared monolithic dependencies create scaling bottlenecks during peak billing periods and constrain independent release velocity. Furthermore, legal practices lack a unified agentic AI layer across their disparate cloud tools, preventing fee earners from leveraging shared intelligence, standardized security policies, and contextual AI assistants directly within high-traffic surfaces like Microsoft Outlook and Word.

## Solution

We are decomposing PCMS, Time Capture, and Legal Forms into modular, enterprise-grade microservices deployment. By standardizing on the IQ platform and achieving IQ Agentic certification, the microservices ecosystem exposes Microsoft 365 MCP endpoints—enabling fee earners to seamlessly orchestrate matter management, automated time capture, and legal form generation from within their primary Microsoft 365 workspaces.
