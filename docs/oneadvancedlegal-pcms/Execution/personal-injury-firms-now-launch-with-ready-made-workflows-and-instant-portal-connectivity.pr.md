# Personal injury workflows

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Practice Case Management System (PCMS)
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Personal Injury Firms Now Launch with Ready-Made Workflows and Instant Portal Connectivity

## Summary
Personal injury law firms can now accelerate their PCMS setup with pre-built workflows tailored to personal injury practice, including direct integrations to external portals such as the Road Traffic Accident (RTA) portal. Ready-made configurations enable firms to achieve operational value immediately, eliminating weeks of manual workflow design and reducing time-to-first-matter from months to days.

## Problem Statement
Personal injury firms face significant onboarding delays when implementing new case management systems. Without pre-configured workflows aligned to their specific practice area, firms must manually design processes, test integrations, and train staff on custom setups—delaying the realization of system value and extending their transition from legacy systems. Portal connectivity, particularly with RTA and similar external case management platforms, requires additional custom development, further lengthening deployment timelines and increasing implementation costs.

## Solution
PCMS now provides personal injury law firms with industry-specific, pre-built workflow templates designed around the unique lifecycle of personal injury claims—from intake through settlement and claims management. These ready-made workflows include pre-configured integrations to the Road Traffic Accident (RTA) portal and other relevant external systems, eliminating custom development. Firms can deploy immediately, configure in hours rather than weeks, and begin case intake within days, dramatically accelerating time-to-value.

## Internal Notes
This release targets mid-market personal injury firms (20–50 fee earners) and addresses a key deployment bottleneck identified in post-implementation surveys. RTA portal integration is the highest-priority external system for this vertical; other portals (CGU, Ageas, etc.) may be added in subsequent releases based on firm demand. Track adoption velocity and time-to-first-case metrics as leading indicators of release success.
