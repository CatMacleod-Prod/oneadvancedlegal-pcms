# Open banking for accounts

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Accounts Management
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Enterprise firms now automate client money management with Open Banking-enabled accounts reconciliation

## Summary
We're releasing Open Banking integration for the Accounts module, enabling larger legal practices to automatically reconcile client money movements in real time. This completes the end-to-end accounts capability built over previous quarters, delivering enterprise-scale automation and confidence in handling client funds across jurisdictions.

## Problem Statement
Mid-market and large legal practices manage substantial client money flows across multiple bank accounts, yet reconciliation remains a manual, error-prone process. Finance teams spend hours matching transactions, verifying client ledger balances, and confirming compliance with Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) and Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules. This creates operational friction, audit risk, and delays in identifying discrepancies—particularly critical when managing funds for conveyancing, probate, and family matters at scale.

## Solution
Open Banking connectivity now allows practices to securely link their client bank accounts directly into the PCMS Accounts module. Real-time transaction feeds automatically match against client ledgers, flag reconciliation exceptions, and provide complete audit trails. Finance teams gain instant visibility into cash flow, reduce manual data entry, and strengthen regulatory compliance across both English and Scots law jurisdictions.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Secure Open Banking API integration with client bank accounts | Eliminate manual bank statement entry and reconciliation loops | Recover 5–8 hours per week per finance team member |
| Automated transaction matching against client ledgers | Detect discrepancies and client money breaches in real time | Reduce audit risk and strengthen SRA/SLAB compliance posture |
| Dual-jurisdiction reconciliation workflow (SRA and Law Society of Scotland rules) | Apply jurisdiction-specific validation rules automatically | Maintain regulatory confidence across England, Wales, and Scotland |
| Real-time cash position dashboard and exception alerts | Monitor client fund health without manual queries | Enable faster decision-making on fund movements and client communications |

Contact your Account Manager or visit our Help Centre to enable Open Banking for your practice. Existing customers can activate the feature immediately in Settings > Bank Connections. New practices can configure Open Banking during onboarding to start automating reconciliation from day one.
