# Press Release

## Headline
PCMS Launches Out-of-the-Box Conveyancing Accelerator Pack to Automate End-to-End Purchase, Sale, and Re-mortgage Workflows

## Summary
The Practice Case Management System (PCMS) introduces the Conveyancing Accelerator Pack, delivering turnkey, standardized end-to-end legal workflows for residential Purchase, Sale, and Re-mortgage transactions. Built for mid-market and large law firms across England and Wales, the Accelerator Pack unifies matter progression, client onboarding, Laserforms, statutory search ordering, SDLT calculations, financial ledgers, and Land Registry filings into a single automated operational pipeline.

## Problem Statement
Residential conveyancing teams lose 1.5 to 2 hours of billable capacity per fee earner every day navigating fragmented, manual processes and disparate point solutions. Fee earners and legal assistants are bogged down by repetitive data entry, manual document assembly, uncoordinated milestone tracking, and separate portal logins for searches, tax submissions, and Land Registry filings—leading to transaction delays, compliance oversights, and fee-earner burnout.

## Solution
The Conveyancing Accelerator Pack delivers fully configured, out-of-the-box workflow pipelines covering the complete matter lifecycle from instruction through post-completion. It features automated pre-exchange and completion condition gates, real-time SDLT/LTT calculation and digital form submission, direct integration with InfoTrack and Lender Exchange for search and Land Registry (OS1/AP1) filings, and centralized financial ledgers that automatically generate structured completion statements and posting entries.

## Customer Impact

| Key Features | Key Benefits | Value & Impact Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Turnkey Purchase, Sale & Re-mortgage Workflows | Ready-to-deploy, standardized process paths covering matter intake to post-completion | Eliminates manual workflow setup and accelerates matter throughput by 1.5 to 2 hours per fee earner daily |
| Embedded Forms & Tax Calculation Engine | In-workflow Laserforms, Hub submissions, and automated SDLT/LTT liability calculations | Cuts form preparation and filing overhead by up to 70% while eliminating standalone tooling costs |
| Automated Checklists & Milestone Gateways | Dynamic pre-exchange and pre-completion validation criteria with automatic progression | Enforces strict regulatory compliance and prevents completion-day oversights or missed milestones |
| Integrated Partner & Land Registry Filings | Seamless in-app search ordering, Lender Exchange verification, and Land Registry OS1/AP1 filings | Removes manual portal context-switching and provides real-time tracking of statutory searches and title registrations |
| Unified Conveyancing Finance & Postings | Centralized transaction ledgers with structured completion statements and automated monies postings | Eliminates manual ledger reconciliation, expedites completion funds distribution, and protects cashier capacity |
