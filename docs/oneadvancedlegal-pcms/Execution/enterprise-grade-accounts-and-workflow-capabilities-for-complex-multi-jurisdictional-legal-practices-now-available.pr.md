# Accounts and workflow depth for larger firms

## Headline
Enterprise-scale transaction management and bulk accounts automation now available for high-volume legal practices

## Summary
We are releasing high-volume transaction management and bulk accounting automation capabilities designed specifically for mid-market and large legal firms managing heavy matter pipelines. These new capabilities enable legal practices operating 100+ concurrent fee earners to process massive volumes of client-to-office transfers, bulk reconciliations, and standardized matter workflows at peak velocity without system lag or manual overhead.

## Problem Statement
High-volume legal practices handling hundreds of active transactions daily face severe operational bottlenecks across cashiering and case administration. Manual one-by-one ledger postings, slow batch reconciliations, and disconnected document filing create administrative backlogs that delay completions, increase errors, and erode fee-earner realization. Legacy systems cannot sustain the concurrent throughput required during peak billing and completion windows, forcing firms to add administrative headcount just to keep pace with transaction load.

## Solution
The enhanced PCMS delivers an enterprise-grade transaction engine featuring high-throughput bulk ledger processing, automated Open Banking data feeds with instant rule-based matching, and high-velocity workflow pipelines. Finance teams and fee earners can execute batch client-to-office transfer requisitions with multi-tier digital sign-offs, automate high-volume invoice processing, and drive standardized matter lifecycles at scale—eliminating administrative lag across 100+ concurrent users on a unified cloud platform.
