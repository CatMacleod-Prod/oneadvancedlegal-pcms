# Debt recovery agents

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Debt Recovery Workflow Agents
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Intelligent Debt Recovery Agents Now Automate Case Workflows While Teams Retain Control of Critical Decisions

## Summary
Debt recovery teams now have AI-driven agents working seamlessly within their Q3 debt recovery workflows, automating repetitive case handling steps—portal interactions, third-party communications, and administrative tasks—while keeping human decision-makers in full control of outcomes that matter. Teams can now process significantly more cases without expanding headcount, and cases move through workflows faster with fewer manual handoffs.

## Problem Statement
Debt recovery teams today spend the majority of their time on high-volume, repetitive tasks: navigating client portals, coordinating with third parties, updating case statuses, and managing routine communications. These manual activities consume fee-earner capacity without adding strategic value, limiting the number of cases a team can handle per period and slowing case progression. Even with skilled staff, case throughput remains constrained by available headcount, and recovery timelines stretch unnecessarily.

## Solution
Debt recovery agents integrate directly into existing Q3 debt recovery workflows, automating the operational steps that previously required manual intervention. Agents handle portal interactions, third-party communications, and routine case administration with human oversight built in—team members retain full authority over settlement decisions, payment terms, escalations, and any outcome that affects client relationships or recovery strategy. This hybrid human-agent model removes bottlenecks in case movement while preserving the judgment and accountability that matter most.
