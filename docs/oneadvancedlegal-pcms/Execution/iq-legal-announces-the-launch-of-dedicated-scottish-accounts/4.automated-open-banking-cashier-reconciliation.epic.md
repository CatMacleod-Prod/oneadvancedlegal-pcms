# Automated Open Banking Cashier Reconciliation

## Strategic Context
Legal cashiering teams spend substantial hours daily manually matching bank statements against client and office ledgers, a process vulnerable to human error, missed postings, and delayed identification of unallocated funds. By integrating real-time Open Banking feeds with automated transaction matching algorithms, PCMS eliminates manual reconciliation friction, accelerates month-end close from days to hours, and ensures continuous compliance with daily client account reconciliation rules.

## Success Criteria
- 90%+ automated matching rate for routine client and office bank transactions
- Reduction in cashier daily bank reconciliation cycle time from 2 hours to under 20 minutes
- Zero unreconciled bank items exceeding 48 hours without automated escalation

## Scope

### In Scope
- Direct Open Banking API integration for automated daily transaction ingest
- Intelligent rule-based and reference-based transaction matching engine
- Cashier exception management workspace for manual review and split-posting of unmatched items

### Out of Scope
- Direct execution of outbound payment transfers (defer to Payment Automation epic)
- Non-UK Open Banking banking protocols and standards

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN Open Banking bank statement feeds are ingested, the Reconciliation Engine SHALL automatically match transactions against open ledger entries based on amount, date, and reference.

WHERE an exact match is identified with confidence score >= 95%, the Reconciliation Engine SHALL auto-stage the entry for single-click cashier reconciliation.

IF an incoming transaction cannot be matched to an existing matter ledger, THEN the Reconciliation Engine SHALL route the transaction to the Unallocated Funds queue and alert the cashiering team.

WHEN a cashier confirms a reconciliation batch, the Reconciliation Engine SHALL update bank balances, flag cleared items, and generate the daily reconciliation audit statement.

## Business Rules
- Client account reconciliations must be retained immutably for a minimum statutory period of 7 years.
- Unallocated client receipts remaining unmatched after 48 hours must automatically trigger an escalation notice to the compliance officer.

## Dependencies
- Open Banking API aggregator connectivity
- Core PCMS General & Client Ledger modules
