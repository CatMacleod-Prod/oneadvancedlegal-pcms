# Dual-Jurisdiction Compliance Markers

## Strategic Context
Multi-jurisdictional law firms operating across Scotland and England & Wales face conflicting accounting and regulatory obligations between the Law Society of Scotland and the SRA. Operating without explicit jurisdiction tagging risks applying incorrect rules, producing flawed compliance submissions, and failing regulatory audits. Dual-jurisdiction compliance markers tag matters, ledgers, and transactions to guarantee appropriate rule enforcement and clear jurisdictional auditability.

## Success Criteria
- 100% of matters and accounting ledgers explicitly tagged with applicable regulatory jurisdiction
- Zero cross-jurisdictional compliance conflicts or unmapped regulatory workflows
- Real-time visual compliance badges and regulatory filters across all financial screens

## Scope

### In Scope
- Matter-level and ledger-level jurisdiction tagging (Law Society of Scotland, SRA, Dual/Cross-Border)
- Context-aware validation routing based on matter jurisdiction marker
- Visual compliance badges, indicators, and filtering in cashiering interfaces

### Out of Scope
- Multi-currency forex ledger conversion (defer to International Finance release)
- Automated cross-border legal advice workflows outside financial accounting

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a new matter is created, the PCMS Compliance Module SHALL require selection of a primary regulatory jurisdiction (Scotland, England & Wales, or Dual).

WHERE a matter is flagged with Scottish jurisdiction, the PCMS Financial Interface SHALL display the Scottish Compliance Marker badge on all associated ledgers.

IF a cashier attempts to apply an SRA-specific rule exception to a Scottish-marked ledger, THEN the Compliance Module SHALL reject the action and display a jurisdictional mismatch warning.

WHEN generating financial compliance reports, the Reporting Engine SHALL filter and group ledger transactions by their assigned jurisdiction marker.

## Business Rules
- Cross-border matters flagged as Dual must apply the stricter of the two accounting constraints unless an explicit statutory carve-out is selected.
- Changing a matter's jurisdiction marker on an active ledger requires partner-level authorization and immutable audit logging.

## Dependencies
- Core PCMS Matter Intake & Setup workflows
- Law Society of Scotland Accounts Rules Engine epic
