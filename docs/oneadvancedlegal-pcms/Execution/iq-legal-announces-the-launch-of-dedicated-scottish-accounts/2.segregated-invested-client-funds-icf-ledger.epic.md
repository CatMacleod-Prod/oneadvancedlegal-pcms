# Segregated Invested Client Funds (ICF) Ledger

## Strategic Context
Law Society of Scotland regulations mandate specific accounting treatment and strict segregation for Invested Client Funds (ICF) held on behalf of clients. Current generic ledger configurations require complex manual spreadsheet workarounds to track interest, term deposits, and segregated accounts. Delivering a dedicated, native ICF sub-ledger ensures strict regulatory segregation, automates interest allocation, and reduces ICF reconciliation time by over 40%.

## Success Criteria
- Dedicated ICF ledger views operating in parallel with standard Scottish general client accounts
- 40%+ reduction in month-end ICF reconciliation and interest calculation time
- 100% accurate interest attribution and audit tracking across designated deposit accounts

## Scope

### In Scope
- Native ICF account creation, linking, and segregation per matter
- Automated interest calculation, distribution, and ledger posting for invested funds
- Dedicated ICF statements, trial balances, and regulatory reporting views

### Out of Scope
- Direct banking investment execution or money market trading capabilities
- Non-interest-bearing general client accounts (handled in standard PCMS ledger)

## Acceptance Criteria
WHEN a designated deposit account is created for a Scottish matter, the ICF Ledger SHALL link the account to the matter's segregated client sub-ledger.

WHEN bank interest is received on an ICF account, the ICF Ledger SHALL calculate gross and net interest and post the amounts to the respective matter ledger.

IF an ICF withdrawal request exceeds available cleared deposit funds, THEN the ICF Ledger SHALL reject the transaction with an insufficient funds notice.

WHERE funds are transferred between general client account and ICF, the ICF Ledger SHALL record offsetting double-entry balancing transactions with mandatory audit notes.

## Business Rules
- All ICF accounts must maintain separate identification numbers distinct from pooled client accounts.
- Interest earned on ICF accounts must be allocated strictly to the designating matter ledger in accordance with Law Society of Scotland Rule 6 requirements.
