# Agentic conveyancing workflows

## Release Information
**Product/Module:** Practice Case Management System (PCMS) – Conveyancing Automation
**Version:** Q4 FY26

## Headline
Conveyancing teams now deploy AI agents to handle routine transaction tasks, freeing fee earners to focus on high-judgement work.

## Summary
Agentic conveyancing workflows automate repetitive, rule-based tasks within the transaction lifecycle—such as ordering searches and managing document requests—allowing transactions to progress with minimal manual intervention. Fee earners reclaim time spent on administrative coordination and redirect their expertise toward legal analysis, client strategy, and matters requiring professional judgement.

## Problem Statement
Conveyancing teams today spend substantial non-billable hours on routine, sequential tasks: ordering searches from multiple providers, chasing missing documents, updating client on transaction status, scheduling completions, and coordinating with third parties. These manual handoffs create bottlenecks, introduce human error, delay transaction closure, and pull fee earners away from high-value legal work. Mid-market and large practices struggle to scale conveyancing throughput without proportionally increasing headcount.

## Solution
PCMS now embeds agentic workflows that autonomously orchestrate common conveyancing tasks within the transaction flow. Agents follow defined rules and legal guardrails to order searches, track document receipt, update transaction status, and escalate issues requiring human judgment. Fee earners configure workflows once, then focus on client advice, contract review, and completion—letting agents handle the coordination overhead. Transactions move forward continuously, handoffs disappear, and fee earners concentrate on work that demands their expertise.
